## Overview

A breakaway warlord alliance holding the Ashvale highlands of Kestrel II, built from a Council militia regiment that walked away when New Kestrel City cut highland funding to pay for Port Kestrel's defenses. Fights out of a fortress built into a Mandate-era bunker and has no intention of ever bending the knee to the Council again.

## Goal

- Planetary Seizure — depose the Kestrel Corporate Council and become the legitimate government of Kestrel II.

## Assets

- [[Guerrilla Populace]] — highland partisans and irregulars loyal to Ashvale
- [[Zealots]] — hardened veterans of the original breakaway regiment, willing to fight to the death

## Leadership & Members

- **Warlord** — [[Warlord Rurik Ashvale]], former Council militia commander
- **Infiltrator** — [[Corporal Nyssa Thorne]], embedded among Council dockworkers at the Anchorage on [[Vesper Hollow]]
- A regiment's worth of highland fighters and their families, genuinely convinced they're fighting for Ashvale's survival

## Territory & Holdings

- Ashvale Hold (fortified seat of power, built into a Mandate-era bunker)
- The Ashvale highlands continent, contested at the edges
- [[Ashvale's Answer]] — a captured Council gravtank, the Coalition's single most feared asset on the ground
- No fixed holdings at [[Vesper System]], but a single embedded agent watching the Council's back-channel supply relay there

## Relationships & Rivals

- At war with the [[Kestrel Corporate Council]], led by [[Governor Lian Osk]]
- Buys weapons from the [[Red Veil Cartel]] via [[Captain Vashti Kael]], though Ashvale doesn't fully trust them

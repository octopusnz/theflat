## Overview

"Dash" Corr runs Red Veil's Driftfield operation on Vashti Kael's authority — salvage on the surface, smuggling underneath. Ex-Mandate military like most of Kael's inner circle, Corr treats the wreck field like a personal fiefdom and has little patience for independent scavengers who don't take the hint to move on.

## Appearance & Personality

- Broad-shouldered and scarred, wears salvaged void armor plating like a badge of rank
- Blunt and businesslike, more comfortable giving orders than negotiating
- Fiercely loyal to Kael personally, less so to the Cartel as an institution

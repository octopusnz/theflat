## Overview

Nominally just a customs officer, Voss actually runs the Council's entire back-channel supply relay through the Anchorage — a lifeline to New Kestrel City that doesn't have to run past the Ashvale Coalition's lines. She reports directly to Governor Osk and to almost nobody else, which suits her fine.

## Appearance & Personality

- Precise and unflappable, with a customs officer's gift for making paperwork sound like small talk
- Runs the listening post on a shoestring and knows it; resents Council command for expecting more than they fund
- Quietly contemptuous of the other factions using the Anchorage, but pragmatic enough to work around them rather than against them

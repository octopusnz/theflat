## Overview

Captain of the Nightshade Vow and the Red Veil Cartel's point woman for the Kestrel run, Kael has made a fortune keeping both sides of the Kestrel II civil war armed and fighting. She's not loyal to either faction — only to the profit margin the war keeps generating.

## Appearance & Personality

- Former Mandate Remnant Surveyors navigator, still wears a modified Surveyor flight jacket with the insignia cut off
- Cold and businesslike; treats every negotiation like a transaction, including threats
- Keeps her crew fiercely loyal through generous cuts and brutal discipline

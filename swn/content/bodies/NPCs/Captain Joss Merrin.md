## Overview

Merrin started running the Vesper Reach corridor when the direct routes into Kestrel got too dangerous for an unarmed free trader to risk. Now the Wayward Cairn's regular circuit has made Merrin one of the few people who understands just how many factions are quietly using Vesper Hollow as a back door.

## Appearance & Personality

- Easygoing and talkative, the kind of captain who remembers everyone's name and asks after their family
- Fiercely protective of the Association's neutrality, having seen firsthand what happens to traders who pick a side
- Better informed about what's really going on in Vesper System than almost anyone gives credit for

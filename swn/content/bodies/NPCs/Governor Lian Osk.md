## Overview

The nominal head of the Kestrel Corporate Council, Osk inherited a losing war and a crumbling mandate. She is not a soldier or a warlord — she's a career administrator trying to hold a fracturing planetary government together with trade concessions, promises, and increasingly desperate backroom deals.

## Appearance & Personality

- Sharp, exhausted, and precise; dresses in Council formalwear even during air raids
- Pragmatic to a fault — will deal with pirates, mercenaries, or worse if it keeps New Kestrel City standing
- Privately doubts the Council can win, but won't say so publicly

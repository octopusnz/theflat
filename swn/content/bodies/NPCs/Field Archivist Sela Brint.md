## Overview

Dispatched by Archivist Renn Toll to investigate why Vesper Reach has grown so unreliable, Brint found her answer within weeks: a dying Mandate relay array anchored to one of Vesper's Shroud's moons, its systems failing after centuries without maintenance. What she's found since has raised more questions than it answered.

## Appearance & Personality

- Methodical to the point of obsession, rarely without a data slate recording every reading
- More at ease around old Mandate hardware than around people
- Growing increasingly uneasy about how much of the array's original purpose remains unclear

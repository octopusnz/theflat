## Overview

Sent through the Reach months ago posing as a Meridian refugee, Thorne has worked her way into the Anchorage's dockworker crews and now has better visibility into the Council's supply relay than anyone in Ashvale Hold expected. She reports back irregularly, whenever a trustworthy courier passes through.

## Appearance & Personality

- Unremarkable by design — plain, quiet, forgettable in a room full of louder refugees
- Genuinely believes in the Coalition's cause, but has grown wary of how long she's been out of contact with Ashvale Hold
- Has started making her own calls about what to report and what to sit on, which would trouble her superiors if they knew

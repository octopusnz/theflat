## Overview

A Council gravtank captured whole during an early ambush on a supply convoy through the highland passes — the only one of three taken that day still running. Repainted in Coalition colors and kept alive by scavenged parts and stubborn maintenance crews, it's the single most feared asset either side of the Kestrel II civil war fields on the ground.

## Crew & Passengers

- **Driver/Commander** — Sergeant Yuli Kray, a former Council armor crewman who defected with the regiment
- Two gunners, both highland volunteers trained up from scratch since none of Ashvale's original crews survived the ambush

## Cargo & Notable Gear

- Coalition markings painted directly over the original Council insignia, still faintly visible underneath
- A dwindling stock of TL5 ammunition and spare parts — nothing on Kestrel II can manufacture replacements

## History

- Captured during an ambush on a Council supply convoy moving through the highland passes early in the war
- Has anchored at least two major Coalition victories since, cementing its reputation on both sides

## Overview

The primary world of the Kestrel System and the reason the system matters at all. Kestrel II sits at the crossroads of three jump routes, and Port Kestrel in high orbit has grown into one of the busiest free ports on this stretch of the frontier. The world itself is currently split by an escalating civil war between the old Corporate Council and a breakaway warlord coalition, making the ground below far less safe than the trade lanes above.

## Geography & Environment

- Two continents: Meridian (Council-held, industrial/coastal) and Ashvale (warlord-held, inland highlands)
- Temperate climate with heavy seasonal storms over the equatorial ocean
- Meridian's coastline is dotted with old orbital-elevator anchor ruins from the Mandate era

## Settlements

- **New Kestrel City** (Meridian) — Council capital, ~1.2 million, orbital elevator terminus
- **Dawnreach** (Meridian) — secondary port city, industrial and shipbuilding
- **Ashvale Hold** (Ashvale) — warlord coalition's fortified seat of power
- Numerous unaligned mining and farming settlements caught between the two factions

## Factions & Notable NPCs

- **[[Kestrel Corporate Council]]** — nominal planetary government, losing ground; based in New Kestrel City
- **[[Ashvale Coalition]]** — warlord alliance seeking independence from Council rule; based in Ashvale Hold
- **[[Kestrel Free Traders Association]]** — neutral merchant guild trying to keep Port Kestrel open to both sides
- **[[Red Veil Cartel]]** — smugglers exploiting the chaos to move contraband and weapons
- [[Governor Lian Osk]] — Council administrator, increasingly desperate to broker outside intervention
- [[Warlord Rurik Ashvale]] — Coalition leader, former Council militia commander

## Trade & Economy

- Primary exports: refined ore from the Shattered Belt, agricultural goods from Meridian
- Port Kestrel functions as the system's economic lifeline; both factions tacitly avoid disrupting it
- War has driven up prices on weapons, medical supplies, and spike drive parts

## Points of Interest

- The abandoned orbital elevator anchors — pretech ruins worth investigating
- New Kestrel City's underlevels, now a haven for refugees and black marketeers
- Ashvale Hold's mountain fortress, built into an old Mandate bunker complex

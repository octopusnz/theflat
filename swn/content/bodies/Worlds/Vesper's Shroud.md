## Overview

A banded gas giant wrapped in a faint, ash-colored ring, easy to miss next to Kestrel III's brighter cousin back home. What matters isn't the planet — it's the derelict Mandate relay array anchored to one of its moons, a jump-calibration station that once helped stabilize transit through Vesper Reach and is now, after centuries of neglect, slowly failing. That failure is the true cause of the Reach's growing unreliability.

## Geography & Environment

- A dim, ash-banded gas giant with a faint ring system, far less striking than Kestrel III's
- A handful of small moons; the array is anchored to the largest, a cratered rock locals call the Cairn
- No stable approach without accounting for the array's failing navigation beacons, which throw stray ships' instruments off without warning

## Settlements

- None. The array was never meant to be inhabited — only tended, and it hasn't been tended in a very long time

## Factions & Notable NPCs

- [[Mandate Remnant Surveyors]] — [[Field Archivist Sela Brint]] is stationed here investigating the array's decline before it fails completely or draws scavenger attention
- [[Red Veil Cartel]] — avoids the array itself but uses the navigational dead zones it creates to slip patrols near The Driftfield

## Trade & Economy

- None directly, though the array's instability is indirectly responsible for a great deal of Vesper Reach's smuggling traffic — patrols give the area a wide berth

## Points of Interest

- The Vesper Array — a sprawling Mandate-era relay and jump-calibration station built into the Cairn, its outer sections open to vacuum and its core still drawing power from a source nobody has identified

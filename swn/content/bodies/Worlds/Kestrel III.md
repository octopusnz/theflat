## Overview

A banded gas giant with a large, brilliant ring system — not a place anyone lives, but the reason half of Kestrel's current troubles exist. One of Kestrel III's moons hides a sealed Mandate-era installation, and the pretech cache inside it is the true source of the military-grade weapons and spike drive components flooding into the Kestrel II civil war.

## Geography & Environment

- A banded gas giant with a prominent, richly-colored ring system, easily visible from Kestrel II on a clear night
- A cluster of small moons orbit within and beyond the rings — mostly airless rock, unremarkable except for one
- No stable orbital infrastructure; the rings make close approach hazardous without a skilled pilot

## Settlements

- None. No permanent population, no registered claims — which is exactly why it's gone unnoticed this long

## Factions & Notable NPCs

- **[[Red Veil Cartel]]** — quietly extracting weapons and spike drive components from the vault, disguising the runs as Shattered Belt mining traffic
- **[[Mandate Remnant Surveyors]]** — want the site studied and recovered intact before it's stripped or weaponized further

## Trade & Economy

- No legitimate trade. All economic activity here is the Red Veil Cartel's black-market extraction operation

## Points of Interest

- A sealed Mandate-era vault on one of the moons, the source of the pretech cache — exact moon and access route known only to [[Captain Vashti Kael]] and a handful of her crew

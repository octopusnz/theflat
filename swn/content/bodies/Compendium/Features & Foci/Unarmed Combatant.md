Your empty hands are more dangerous than knives and guns in the grip of the less gifted. Your unarmed attacks are counted as [[Melee|melee]] weapons when it comes to binding up opponents wielding rifles and similar long arms, though you need at least one hand free to do so.

Level 1: Gain [[Punch|Punch]] as a bonus skill. Your unarmed attacks become more dangerous as your [[Punch|Punch]] skill increases. At level-0, they do 1d6 damage. At level-1, they do 1d8 damage. At level-2 they do 1d10, level-3 does 1d12, and level-4 does 1d12+1. At [[Punch|Punch]]-1 or better, they have the Shock quality equal to your [[Punch|Punch]] skill against AC 15 or less. While you normally add your [[Punch|Punch]] skill level to any unarmed damage, don’t add it twice to this Shock damage.
Level 2: You [[Know|know]] locks and twists that use powered servos against their wearer. Your unarmed attacks count as TL4 weapons for the purpose of overcoming advanced armors. Even on a miss with a [[Punch|Punch]] attack, you do an unmodified 1d6 damage.

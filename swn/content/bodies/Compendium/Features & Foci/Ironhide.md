Whether through uncanny reflexes, remarkable luck, gengineered skin fibers, or subtle telekinetic shielding, you have natural defenses equivalent to high-quality combat [[Armor|armor]]. The benefits of this focus don’t stack with [[Armor|armor]], though Dexterity or [[Shield|shield]] modifiers apply.

Level 1: You have an innate [[Armor|Armor]] Class of 15 plus half your character level, rounded up.
Level 2: Your abilities are so effective that they render you immune to unarmed attacks or primitive weaponry as if you wore powered [[Armor|armor]].

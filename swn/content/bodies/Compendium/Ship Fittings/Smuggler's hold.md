## Description

Carefully-designed storage space intended to conceal illicit cargo from customs inspection. Each installation of this fitting adds 200 kilograms of [[Cargo space|cargo space]] in a fighter, 2 tons in a frigate, 20 tons in a cruiser, or 200 tons in a capital ship. Cargo in smuggler's hold will never be found by a standard customs inspection. Careful investigation by a suspicious official can find it on a difficulty 10 check using their Wis/[[Notice|Notice]] skill, and a week-long search one step short of disassembly will find it on a difficulty 7 check.

## Description

Armored and stealthed version of cargo lighters, these craft are twice as fast, apply a -3 penalty to tracking and targeting skill checks, and can carry up to one hundred troops or passengers. Many are equipped with assorted [[Heavy|Heavy]] weapons to clear the landing zone, and can be treated as flight-capable gravtaks for purposes of combat. This fitting can be purchased multiple times.

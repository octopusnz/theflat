## Description

Each time this fitting is selected, 10% of the ship's maximum crew gain access to luxury cabins of spaciousness sufficient to please a wealthy star-farer. This fitting comes with the usual zero-gee athletic courts, decorative fountains, fine-dining, and artistic fittings.

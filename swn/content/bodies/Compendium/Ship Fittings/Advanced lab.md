## Description

A lab suitable for investigating alien xenolife, planetary geology, esoteric technology, and other mysteries of the cosmos. The lab contains [[Cold sleep pods|cold sleep pods]] adequate to contain alien samples, viro-shielded research cells, high-energy lasers, and other requisite tools. When used to investigate some pehomenon or object, any applicable skill rolls are improved by +1 for a frigate lab, +2 for a cruiser lab, and +3 for capital ship lab.

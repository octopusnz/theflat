## Description

An extremely rare example of psitech dating from before the Scream, a precognitive nav chamber allows a character with at least [[Precognition|Precognition]]-2 psionic skill to assist in interstellar drills, sensing impending shear alterations before they happen. The navigator automatically succeeds on any spike drill check of difficulty 9 or less. On a failed check, add 2 to the Spike Drive Mishap roll, limiting the potential damage. On drilling in to the destination system, the psychic has expended all Effort in the process.

## Description

The drone is fitted to drop up to two [[Grenade|grenade]]-sized explosives, one or both in lieu of a different weapon attack. Dropping a [[Grenade|grenade]] from more than 30 meters up automatically causes the [[Grenade|grenade]] to scatter from its intended target point.

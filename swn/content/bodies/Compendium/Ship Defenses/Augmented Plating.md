## Description

At the cost of a certain amount of speed and maneuverability, a ship can have its [[Armor|armor]] plating reinforced against glacing hits, gaining a +2 bonus to its AC. This augmentation can decrease a ship's speed below 0, meaning it will be applied as a penalty on all Pilot tests.

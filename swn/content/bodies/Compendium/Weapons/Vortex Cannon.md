## Description

Vortex cannons use controlled gravitic shear planes to cause a target to simply fall apart into component fragments. The cannons are silent in operation, but so [[Heavy|heavy]] and complex that they can only be mounted on gravtanks and other similar dedicated fighting vehicles.

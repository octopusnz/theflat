## Description

Combat rifles are favored by the militaries of tech level 3 worlds, trading some of the often-unnecessary range and penetration of a conventional [[Rifle|rifle]] for a larger ammunition capacity and burst fire capabilities. On more strait-laced worlds such military weaponry is often illegal for civilians to possess.

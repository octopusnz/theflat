## Description

Grenade users always roll to attack AC 10. On a miss, the grenade lands 1d10 meters away from the target in a random direction. Hit or miss, the grenade then explodes for 2d6 damage to all targets within 5 meters. Victims are allowed an Evasion save for half damage. Targets take 1 less point of damage for each point of AC above 14. Grenades can be thrown with the [[Exert|Exert]] skill instead of [[Shoot|Shoot]], if desired.

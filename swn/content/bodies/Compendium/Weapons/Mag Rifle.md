## Description

Mag weaponry involves the magnetic acceleration of metal flechettes, while spike throwers are the [[Shotgun|shotgun]] equivalents of these weapons. Mag ammunition is packaged with integral power supplies, so no additional power cells are necessary to fire these weapons.

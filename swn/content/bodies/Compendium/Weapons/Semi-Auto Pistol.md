## Description

Semi-automatic pistols [[Trade|trade]] some of the reliability of the [[Revolver|revolver]] for a larger magazine size. They tend to be the favorite sidearm for locals on planets that lack the harsh conditions or uncertain maintenance opportunities of a frontier world.

## Description

Unarmed attacks reflect ordinary kicks and punches. Unarmed attacks always add the attacker’s [[Punch|Punch]] skill to damage rolls, unlike other weapons. Kinesis wraps, spiked knuckles, and other small fist weapons may be treated as small advanced or primitive weapons that use the [[Punch|Punch]] skill and add the skill level to their rolled damage, but not to Shock. Such weapons do not augment a hero with the [[Unarmed Combatant|Unarmed Combatant]] focus.

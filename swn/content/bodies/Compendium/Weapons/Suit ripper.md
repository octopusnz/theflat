## Description

Suit rippers are rods with fractal cutting surfaces designed to cripple [[Vacc suit|vacc suit]] auto-repair routines. Every hit with a suit ripper counts as a suit tear on a [[Vacc suit|vacc suit]]-wearing enemy. Unsurprisingly, these weapons are strictly illegal in space environments.

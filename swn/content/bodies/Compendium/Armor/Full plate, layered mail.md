## Description

Primitive [[Armor|armor]] is a catch-all covering all [[Armor|armor]] technology that lacks access to advanced materials and processes. While such [[Armor|armor]] can be very effective in protecting against primitive weaponry, it is useless against advanced TL4 [[Melee|melee]] weapons and firearms of all kinds. Against these weapons, it is treated as AC 10. Note that some planets have native flora or fauna capable of naturally producing extremely effective [[Armor|armor]] materials, ones not susceptible to this limitation

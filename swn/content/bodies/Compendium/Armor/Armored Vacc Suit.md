## Description

An armored [[Vacc suit|vacc suit]] has traded off some comfort and flexibility for additional protective thickness. Aside from functioning as a normal [[Vacc suit|vacc suit]], it has only half the usual chance to tear when hit by an edged weapon or [[Suit ripper|suit ripper]]. The choice between armored or standard suits is a common debate in spacer bars.

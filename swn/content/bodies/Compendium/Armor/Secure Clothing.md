## Description

Secure clothing comes in assorted styles and fashions ranging from casual street wear to haute couture. Normal fabrics and components are replaced with light, flexible [[Armor|armor]] components that are only slightly hindering to the wearer. Only close tactile examination can distinguish secure clothing from ordinary couture.

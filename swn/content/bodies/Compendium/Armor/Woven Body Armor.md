## Description

Woven body [[Armor|armor]] represents the best [[Armor|armor]] that a TL3 world can manufacture, or an up-armored version of [[Security Armor|security armor]] used by TL4 high-threat response teams. The design is significantly more cumbersome, but allows for multiple layers of protection.

## Description

Storm [[Armor|armor]] is a more advanced form of [[Assault Suit|assault suit]], one that can be fabricated only by those worlds that have retained some degree of Mandate-grade technical infrastructure. Aside from functioning as an [[Assault Suit|assault suit]], the storm [[Armor|armor]]’s exo-augments allow the wearer to treat their Strength as 4 points higher for encumbrance purposes. Integral gravitic boosters allow the wearer to leap up to 20 meters as a Move action, either horizontally or vertically, and allow the wearer to fall up to 40 meters without suffering harm. An onboard medical computer can attempt last-ditch stabilization of the wearer if all other efforts fail; when the wearer would normally die from an untreated mortal wound, they can make a Physical save to self-stabilize. Storm [[Armor|armor]] requires the same type B power cell as an [[Assault Suit|assault suit]], and each cell [[Powers|powers]] it for 24 hours.

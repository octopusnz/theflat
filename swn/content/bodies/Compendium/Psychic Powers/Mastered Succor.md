## Description

The biopsion has developed a sophisticated mastery of their core ability, and they no longer need to Commit Effort to activate it, and may use it whenever they wish. The use of additional techniques that augment *[[Psychic Succor|Psychic Succor]]* might still require Effort to be Committed.

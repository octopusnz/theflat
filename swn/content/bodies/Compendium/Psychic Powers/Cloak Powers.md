## Description

The metapsion can conceal their own psychic abilities from metapsionic senses. They must Commit Effort for as long as they wish to cloak their [[Powers|powers]]. While hidden, only a metapsion with equal or higher skill in [[Metapsionics|Metapsionics]] can detect their abilities with their level-0 or level-2 *[[Psychic Refinement|Psychic Refinement]]* abilities. In such cases, an opposed Wis/[[Metapsionics|Metapsionics]] roll is made between the metapsion and the investigator. If the investigator wins, the cloak is pierced, while if the metapsion wins, the investigator’s *[[Psychic Refinement|Psychic Refinement]]* remains oblivious.

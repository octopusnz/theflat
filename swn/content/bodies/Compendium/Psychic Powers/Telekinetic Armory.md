## Description

The adept may Commit Effort as an On Turn action to create both weapons and [[Armor|armor]] out of telekinetic force. These weapons are treated as tech level 4 and act as a [[Rifle|rifle]] or any advanced [[Melee|melee]] weapon. Attack rolls can use either Dexterity, Wisdom, or Constitution modifiers, and may use the [[Telekinesis|Telekinesis]] skill as the combat skill.

[[Armor|Armor]] may be created as part of this power, granting the psychic a base [[Armor|Armor]] Class equal to 15 plus their [[Telekinesis|Telekinesis]] skill level. This [[Armor|armor]] does not stack with conventional [[Armor|armor]], but Dexterity or shields modify it as usual. The gear continues to exist as long as the psychic chooses to leave the Effort committed, and they may be invisible or visible at the psychic’s discretion.

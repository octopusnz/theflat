## Description

The [[Telepath|telepath]] is practiced at opening a *[[Telepathic Contact|Telepathic Contact]]*, and need only Commit Effort for the scene to do so, instead of Committing Effort for the day. If contacting an ally who has practiced the process with the psychic for at least a week, opening the contact normally requires no Effort at all. In both cases, if the [[Telepath|telepath]] chooses to Commit Effort for the day, they can open a *[[Telepathic Contact|Telepathic Contact]]* as an Instant action rather than a Main Action.

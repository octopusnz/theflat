## Description

*[[Psychic Succor|Psychic Succor]]* and other biopsionic techniques that normally require touch contact can now be applied at a distance up to 100 meters, provided the biopsion can see the target with their unaided vision. Hostile [[Powers|powers]] that normally require a hit roll will hit automatically. Each time this technique is used, Effort must be Committed for the scene.

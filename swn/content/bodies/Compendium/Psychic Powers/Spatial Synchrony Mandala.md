## Description

The psychic imprints a particular object or person on their psionic awareness. Provided the object is relatively intact and in range of their *[[Personal Apportation|Personal Apportation]]*, the psychic always knows its exact location and can teleport to within three meters of it with *[[Personal Apportation|Personal Apportation]]* even if it has moved from its original location. Imprinting an object requires an hour’s meditation with it, and only one object can be imprinted at a time. If imprinting on a person, the target must be willing and cooperative to make the imprint. Objects must be at least one kilogram in mass to be effectively tracked.

## Description

While somewhat primitive by pretech standards, this TL5 cybernetic implant allows the wearer to gain a +3 bonus on all saving throws to resist telepathic intrusion. Even if such a save is failed, the user will be aware that they are under [[Telepathic Assault|telepathic assault]], though some forms of mental control may not allow them to say as much until after the influence is ended. The warning does not indicate the source of the influence.

## Description

The standard personal vehicle of most TL4 worlds, though most lower-class citizens rely on public transport in urban areas. Most advanced worlds have planetary transit grids that allow occupants to simply code in a destination and let the car take them there. Where infrastructure or local conditions preclude that convenience, manual controls and Pilot skill can be used.

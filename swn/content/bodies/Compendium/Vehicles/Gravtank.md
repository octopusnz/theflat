## Description

The bulk of a prosperous TL4 world's armored forces. Private ownership is strictly forbidden on most worlds, but "export models" often can be acquired for transport offworld. Gravtanks have armor heavy enough to ignore all damage from conventional small arms, and can only be harmed by Heavy weapons or properly-placed demolitions charges — immune to anything short of TL4 Heavy weapons, while less sophisticated tanks are immune to anything below TL3 Heavy ordnance. Equipped with advanced targeting systems, so all of its weapons can be targeted and fired by a single gunner.

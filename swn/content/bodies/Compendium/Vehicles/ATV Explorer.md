## Description

Rugged vehicles, either wheeled or tracked, and driven by type B power cells or locally-available biofuels. While they can't navigate rough terrain as easily as a grav vehicle, they're designed to take a tremendous amount of punishment before breaking down and support repairs using basic tools and frontier expedients.

## Description

Grapnel launcher: This launcher fires a rope up to forty meters. The rope can bear up to a metric ton of weight, though extremely [[Heavy|heavy]] weights or a precarious hit can cause the grapnel to pull free. A Type A power cell fuels six shots from a launcher.

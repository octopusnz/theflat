## Description

Hush ([[Heal|Heal]]-0): An extremely [[Heavy|heavy]] neurotranquilizer, Hush leaves the subject awake and responsive to simple, untaxing commands. They cannot fight or communicate while affected by Hush, but will walk, eat, sit, and [[Perform|perform]] simple actions as directed by the last person to speak to them. A Hush dose lasts for an hour plus three per [[Heal|Heal]] level of the person applying the stim, and the subject retains no memories while under the effect. A subject must be immobilized or willing to be affected by Hush; violent physical movement immediately after application disrupts the neurological effects.

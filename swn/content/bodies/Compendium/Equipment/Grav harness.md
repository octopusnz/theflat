## Description

Grav harness: This advanced gravitic harness allows clumsy flight at a rate of 20 meters per round as a Main Action. It requires a Type B power cell to fuel it, and the miniaturized electronics are rapacious, consuming the cell in five minutes of operation. It can [[Lift|lift]] up to 200 kilos.

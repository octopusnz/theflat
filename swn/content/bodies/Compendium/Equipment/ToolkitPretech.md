## Description

Toolkits/Pretech: Containing a wide range of necessary tools for a particular skill set, toolkits can handle almost any job that doesn’t require a full-scale shop or lab. These sophisticated pretech tools are necessary for working on advanced TL5 artifacts, and the tools themselves are very rare and difficult to obtain.

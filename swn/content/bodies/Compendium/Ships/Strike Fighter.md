## Description

The standard drill-capable fighter craft of most TL4 polities, this model retains a spike drive instead of replacing it with a cheaper systems drive. While the lone pilot would have to be desperate and heavily drugged to make an entire spike drill solo, it remains an option when no larger craft is available.
This particular model is equipped with a [[Reaper Battery|reaper battery]] for dealing with small, lightly-armored civilian ships. “[[Bomber|Bomber]]”-type fighters usually replace this with a [[Fractal Impact Charge|fractal impact charge]] to threaten larger warships, while fighters built to dogfight their peers favor fitting a [[Multifocal Laser|multifocal laser]].

## Fittings

### [[Atmospheric configuration|Atmospheric Configuration]]

> Mass: 1 | Effect: Can land: frigates and fighters only.

This fitting must be put in place when the ship is built, and cannot be installed on cruiser-class or larger ships. A ship designed for atmospheric flight can land on most solid or aqueous surfaces.

## Description

Sometimes confusingly called a “light cruiser”, a heavyfrigate pushes the engineering parameters of a frigate hull to the maximum available to most TL4 worlds. Equipped with a [[Torpedo Launcher|torpedo launcher]] capable of threatening even cruiser-class enemies, the [[Heavy|heavy]] frigate can intimidate any ship of its weight class. Its [[Heavy|heavy]] crew complement and military [[Drop pod|drop pod]] fitting allow it to quickly dispatch well-armed marines to hot spots throughout a solar system.  However, despite the large weapon loadout, the [[Heavy|heavy]] frigate remains a frigate. Its [[Armor|armor]] is significantly inferior to that of a true cruiser-class warship, and most frigates built for same-class engagements can be a meaningful threat to it. It comes into its own as a bludgeon against the other small warships likely to be fielded by a peer star-faring polity.

## Defenses

### [[Hardened Polyceramic Overlay|Hardened Polyceramic Overlay]]

A complex glazing process can harden the surface of a ship’s [[Armor|armor]] to more effectively shed incoming attacks, decreasing the [[Armor|armor]]-piercing quality of any hit by 5.

## Fittings

### Spike [[Drive-2 upgrade|Drive-2 upgrade]]

> Power: 1 | Mass: 1 | Effect: Upgrade a spike drive to drive-2 rating

A ship can improve its standard-issue drive-1 spike drive with additional phase filters and power throughput refinements. A captain needs buy only the final grade of drive desired. He does not have to buy upgrades sequentially. Drives of rating 4 and higher are generally TL5 artifacts that cannot be built most modern worlds.

### [[Drop pod|Drop Pod]]

> Mass: 2 | Effect: Upgrade a spike drive to drive-2 rating

Armored and stealthed versions of cargo lighters, these craft are twice as fast, apply a -3 penalty to tracking and targeting skill checks, and can carry up to one hundred troops or passengers. Many are equipped with assorted [[Heavy|Heavy]] weapons to clear the landing zone, and can be treated as flight-capable gravtanks for purposes of combat. This fitting can be purchased multiple times.

### [[Fuel scoops|Fuel Scoops]]

> Power: 2 | Mass: 2 | Effect: Ship can scoop fuel from a gas giant or star

[[Fuel scoops|Fuel scoops]] allow for the harvesting and extraction of hydrogen from gas giants or the penumbra of solar bodies. The extraction process requires four days of processing and refinement, but completely refuels the ship. Such fittings are common on explorer craft that cannot expect to find refueling stations.

### Fuel Bunker

> Mass: 1 | Effect: Adds fuel for one more drill between fuelings

Most ships require refueling after each drill jump, no matter the distance. Installing [[Fuel bunkers|fuel bunkers]] allows the ship to carry one additional load of fuel. This fitting can be installed multiple times for ships that wish to minimize fueling.

### [[Armory|Armory]]

> Effect: Weapons and [[Armor|armor]] for the crew

Rather than maintaining lengthy lists of ship equipment, a captain can simply buy an [[Armory|armory]]. Ships so equipped have whatever amounts of TL4 military-grade weaponry and [[Armor|armor]] that a crew might require, and integral maintenance facilities for its upkeep. There is enough gear available to outfit the entire crew for normal use, but giving it away or losing it in use may deplete it.

### [[Ship's locker|Ship's Locker]]

> Effect: General equipment for the crew

Much like an [[Armory|armory]], this option allows a captain to lay in a general supply of equipment likely to be useful to explorers and spacemen. Any TL4 equipment on the gear list can be found in the ship’s locker in amounts commensurate with the ship’s size. A few guns and some basic [[Armor|armor]] might be included as well, but for serious armament an [[Armory|armory]] is required. There is enough gear available to outfit the entire crew for normal use, but giving it away or losing it in use may deplete the locker until it is restocked.

### [[Boarding tubes|Boarding Tubes]]

> Mass: 1 | Effect: Allows boarding of a hostile disabled ship

Armored tubes equipped with laser cutter apertures can be used to forcibly invade a hostile ship, provided the target’s engines have been disabled. Ships without [[Boarding tubes|boarding tubes]] have to send invaders across empty space to either make an assault on a doubtless heavily-guarded airlock or cut their way in through the hull with laser cutters and half an hour of [[Work|work]].

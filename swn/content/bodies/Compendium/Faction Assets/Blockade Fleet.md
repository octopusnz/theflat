## Description

A ragtag lot of corsairs, pirates, privateers, and other deniable [[Assets|assets]]. When they successfully Attack an enemy faction asset, they steal 1d4 FacCreds from the target faction as well. This theft can occur to a faction only once per turn, no matter how many blockade fleets attack. As an action, this asset may also move itself to a world within one hex.

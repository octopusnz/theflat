## Description

Deployed to confuse enemy factions into untimely investments. As an action, the marketers may test Cunning vs. Wealth against a rival faction's asset. If successful, the target faction must immediately pay half the asset's purchase cost, rounded down, or have it become disabled and useless until this price is paid.

## Description

An open or tacit stranglehold on certain vital businesses or resources on a world. As an action, owners of a monopoly may force one other faction with unstealthed [[Assets|assets]] on that world to pay them one FacCred. If the target faction can't pay, they lose one asset of their choice on the world.

## Description

As an action, the owning faction can roll 1d6 for a Postech Industry asset. On a 1, one FacCred is lost, on a 2-4 one FacCred is earned, and a 5-6 returns two FacCreds. If money is lost and no resources are available to pay it, the Postech Industry is destroyed. Roll: [[1d6]]

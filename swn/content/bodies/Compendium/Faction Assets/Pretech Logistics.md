## Description

These [[Assets|assets]] represent caches, [[Smugglers|smugglers]], or internal research and salvage programs. As an action, a pretech logistics asset allows the owner to buy one Force asset on that world that requires up to tech level 5 to purchase. This asset costs half again as many FacCreds as usual, rounded up. Only one asset can be purchased per turn.

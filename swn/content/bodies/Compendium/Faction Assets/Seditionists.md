## Description

These asset sap a target's loyalty and will to obey. For a cost of 1d4 FacCreds, the Seditionists can attach themselves to an enemy asset. Until they attach to a different asset or no longer share the same planet, the affected asset cannot attack. If the asset is destroyed, the Seditionists [[Survive|survive]]. Roll: [[1d4]]

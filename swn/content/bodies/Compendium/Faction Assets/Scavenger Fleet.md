## Description

These rag-tag armadas bring enormous technical and mercantile resources to their patrons, along with a facility with [[Heavy|heavy]] guns. As an action, a Scavenger Fleet can be moved to any world within three hexes. Scavenger Fleets cost 2 FacCreds a turn in maintenance.

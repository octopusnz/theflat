## Overview

A free merchant running the Vesper Reach corridor on a steady loop, carrying cargo, mail, and the occasional passenger between Vesper Hollow and Port Kestrel. Merrin keeps it scrupulously legal, which has made the Cairn one of the few ships every faction in Vesper System trusts enough to leave alone.

## History

- Purchased secondhand by the Free Traders Association specifically to open the Vesper corridor once the direct Kestrel routes grew too dangerous
- Has never missed a scheduled run, a point of quiet pride for its crew

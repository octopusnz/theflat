## Overview

A former frontier-navy patrol boat, stripped of its markings and repainted matte black, the Widow's Toll is Red Veil's enforcement arm in the Driftfield — fast enough to run down scavengers who won't take a hint, armored enough to shrug off the debris field's hazards.

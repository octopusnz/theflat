## Overview

The system at the far end of Vesper Reach, dimmer and quieter than Kestrel under its banked orange star. Vesper has no war of its own — what it has is a dying Mandate relay array whose slow collapse is the real reason the Reach has grown so unreliable, and a small waystation now quietly used by nearly every faction with an interest in Kestrel's war as a back channel nobody else is watching too closely.

## Space Stations & Facilities

- **The Anchorage** — orbital/surface waystation over Vesper Hollow; the system's only real settlement and its only law, such as it is

## Jump Routes

- [[Vesper Reach]] — 4 days back to [[Kestrel System]]; the route's growing unreliability traces back to the failing Mandate array on [[Vesper's Shroud]]
- The Silent Span — unexplored beyond the jump point

## Factions Present

- [[Kestrel Corporate Council]] — small listening post at the Anchorage, a supply back channel that bypasses Ashvale-contested space
- [[Ashvale Coalition]] — a single embedded infiltrator watching Council traffic
- [[Kestrel Free Traders Association]] — an alternate trade corridor now that the direct Kestrel run has grown dangerous
- [[Red Veil Cartel]] — salvage and smuggling operation working The Driftfield and fencing goods through the Anchorage
- [[Mandate Remnant Surveyors]] — investigating the failing array on [[Vesper's Shroud]] before it either collapses or gets stripped by scavengers

## Points of Interest

- The Vesper Array, the derelict Mandate relay anchored to one of Shroud's moons — see [[Vesper's Shroud]]
- The Anchorage's underlevels, a loose gray market where every faction present pretends not to notice the others
